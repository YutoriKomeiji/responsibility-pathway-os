Reproducibility

The maps have public source and a pinned renderer.

The canonical diagram inputs live under visuals/archify/. CI renders them with the forked Archify revision 0853a805003514776bef3593ecca091409828902. A change to either RPOS diagram sources or the pinned renderer route must pass validation again.

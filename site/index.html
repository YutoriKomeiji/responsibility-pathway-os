RPOS is an independently engineered, open-source Responsibility Pathway OS. Its Python/SQLite runtime keeps Human Gate, operational authority, dispatch, external-effect verification, uncertainty, repair, explicit resumption, and completion connected as executable state; its Lean 4 Formal Assurance Surface machine-checks selected responsibility invariants and links them to Python runtime tests.

The problem

A successful API response is not the same thing as a verified effect.

Authority is contextual

RPOS binds authorization to the actor, operation, action, evidence, context, time, target, and exact effect instead of treating permission as a timeless boolean.

Unknown stays unknown

If an external action may have happened but cannot be verified, RPOS does not silently convert ambiguity into success or automatic replay.

Recovery preserves ownership

Restart and reconciliation retain the unresolved pathway and return it to repair or a Human Gate instead of erasing responsibility.

Independent lineage

RPOS is the operating layer of the Responsibility Pathway lineage.

RPOS is independently engineered through Responsibility Pathway Model / Paper → Design → Engineering → Runtime → Operating System. The lineage studies how responsibility remains connected across judgment, authorization, execution, uncertainty, repair, return, resumption, and residual ownership.

RPOS owns operation, not intelligence. Models are replaceable proposal sources; a proposal does not become operational authority merely because a model produced it.

What this statement means

  • the project has its own documented Responsibility Pathway lineage;
  • its runtime and formal artifacts are inspectable in this repository;
  • third-party names, institutions, standards, patents, or media do not transfer authority or proof to RPOS.

Responsibility is a path, not a flag.

Lean 4 Formal Assurance Surface

Six published responsibility invariants are machine-checked and cross-linked to Python runtime tests.

RPOS machine-checks named structural properties including: Human Gate cannot dispatch directly; only VERIFIED may enter COMPLETED; EFFECT_UNKNOWN is not completion; READY_TO_RESUME is not authority; a receipt is not external-effect verification; and a model proposal is not operational authority. The viewer links each risk to its Lean theorem, executable Python evidence, source identity, model scope, and proof ceiling.

What the proof establishes

  • the named invariant holds in its declared Lean model;
  • the corresponding Python behavior is independently exercised by linked runtime tests;
  • the public catalog keeps theorem scope and proof ceiling explicit;
  • formal proof, implementation evidence, and external-effect evidence remain distinct.

Evidence boundary & promotion

Public Alpha is a current evidence state, not a permanent disclaimer.

RPOS separates evidence-limited boundaries that can move from permanent responsibility boundaries that software should not cross by itself. Production-readiness, broader platform support, implementation-wide formal conformance, software-supply-chain trust, and domain-effectiveness claims can advance only when their declared evidence criteria are met and reviewed.

The long-term goal is larger than the current alpha: make responsibility-bearing operational state easier to execute, inspect, test, formally reason about, recover, and return to accountable humans or institutions across real AI-enabled workflows. That is a direction, not a claim of completion.

See promotion criteria

Permanent responsibility boundaries

  • software does not create legal or organizational authority;
  • a receipt is not proof of external effect;
  • governing a pathway does not make a third-party system correct;
  • arbitrary external systems cannot receive a universal exactly-once guarantee without the required contract;
  • abstract formal proofs do not automatically prove the complete runtime or deployment.

# Code of Conduct

RPOS is an engineering and research project. Participation should remain respectful, evidence-oriented, and safe for technical disagreement.

Contributors and reviewers are expected to:

- criticize claims, designs, tests, and evidence rather than people;
- distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis, and verified result;
- disclose uncertainty and conflicting evidence when relevant;
- avoid publishing credentials, private data, or exploit details through ordinary issues;
- avoid using project discussion to misrepresent certification, legal authority, organizational endorsement, or deployment approval.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, or deliberate exposure of secrets are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove participation that materially violates these boundaries. Good-faith criticism, negative results, reproduction failures, and counterexamples are welcome.
